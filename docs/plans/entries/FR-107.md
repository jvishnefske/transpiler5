- [x] FR-107 Pointer-ARRAY struct members (`T *m[N]`) — the real
  root under lwIP's two biggest cascades, identified 2026-08-21
  by peeling the chain rather than reading a diagnostic count.
  CORRECTION FIRST, because this entry exists to fix a mistake
  made twice. FR-102's entry claimed `Netif` (58) and `TcpPcb`
  (64) were cascades of the fn-pointer-component root; its
  delivered verdict corrected that to "the root is `void *state;`
  at netif.h:334". BOTH are wrong. Deleting `void *state` and
  re-probing lwIP changed NOTHING — 583 ported, `Netif` still 71.
  The line-offset reading was simply off: netif.h:334 is
  `void* client_data[LWIP_NETIF_CLIENT_DATA_INDEX_MAX + ...]`, an
  ARRAY OF VOID POINTERS. Two people (the spike and this author)
  mis-read the same line the same way because neither re-derived
  it with `awk`-numbered output; the lesson recorded is that a
  `file:line` from a diagnostic must be printed WITH its line
  number, never counted by eye from a `sed` range.
  THE CHAIN IS EXACTLY TWO LINKS, and both are the same shape.
  Peeling `struct netif` with a two-line probe
  (`#include "lwip/netif.h"` + `struct netif probe;`), patching
  the reported line, and re-probing until it imports:
    1. netif.h:334  `void* client_data[N]`        (array of void*)
    2. stats.h:274  `struct stats_mem *memp[MEMP_MAX]` (array of struct*)
  With both replaced, `struct netif` IMPORTS. Nothing else blocks
  it. Both links are POINTER-ARRAY STRUCT MEMBERS, which is
  therefore one front, not two, and is distinct from both the
  fn-pointer-component front (FR-102, closed) and the `void *`
  PARAMETER front.
  PROPOSED REPRESENTATION, fitting the existing model rather than
  inventing one: CTS-P2 already stores a scalar data-pointer
  member as a plain i64 cursor and rejects at the READ site when
  the target is not statically resolvable. The same discipline
  extends one dimension — a pointer-array member becomes
  `[i64; N]`, the record imports, and any element access whose
  target cannot be resolved keeps a located rejection at the USE.
  That is the project's standing shape (admit the type, reject
  the unresolvable use), and it is what lets a record whose
  pointer-array members are never touched in this TU — exactly
  `client_data` and `memp` in lwIP core — stop gating everything
  that merely NAMES it.
  **VALUE NOT YET MEASURED — and deliberately not guessed.** The
  obvious number is the 71 + 64 = 135 cascade items, but FR-102
  is the standing proof that opening a root does not port what
  sits behind it. An attempt to measure it here was CONTAMINATED
  and is reported as such rather than quoted: replacing the two
  members with `int` broke `tcp.c`'s own compilation, so
  graph_items fell 1030 -> 877 and 153 items simply left the
  denominator. The ported RATE rose (56.6% -> 64.9%) and both
  cascades shrank (`Netif` 71 -> absent, `TcpPcb` 64 -> 25), but
  no honest item delta can be read off a measurement whose
  denominator moved. A clean measurement needs a semantics-
  preserving probe, and getting one is the spike's first job.
  **NOT SPIKED.**
